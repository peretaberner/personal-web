Results and Impact Evaluation of “Treball als Barris de Manresa” – Avaluació de resultats i impacte del Programa Treball als barris de Manresa

Client: Ajuntament de Manresa

My role: co-authorship

Language: Catalan

Summary: The aim of this study is to evaluate the overall impact of the “Treball als Barris” program in the municipality of Manresa, both in terms of employment and social impact. First, a systematic literature review of similar programs is conducted. Second, the study analyzes the employment and social trajectories of participants, how the program interventions have influenced these trajectories, and which profiles have responded best to each intervention. Third, a case study is carried out using focus groups to gain a deeper understanding of the participants’ experiences. Finally, a counterfactual analysis is performed to measure the program’s impact on the participants.

Development of a model for community detection and early intervention in at-risk case – Desarrollo del modelo de detección comunitaria e intervención temprana en casos de situación de riesgo

Client: UNICEF

My role: collaboration within the KSNET team

Language: Spanish

Summary: This work consists of the design of a methodology model for detection and intervention at the local level in situations of risk for children and adolescents from a community, preventive and gender perspective, based on comprehensive support centered on each person and on children’s rights. The objectives of the research are (i) to design the model of intervention in risk situations at the local level, differentiating between the detection model and the model of intervention in a situation of moderate risk (ii) to improve the system of detection and local intervention in risk situations (iii) to generate and disseminate knowledge to facilitate its replicability in other municipalities. The results to be achieved are (i) diagnosis of the situation (ii) map of resources, measures and actions (iii) bank of good practices (iv) proposal of the community and integral intervention model (v) proposal of the community and integral intervention model. For the development of the project, 10 pilot municipalities have been selected to serve as the basis for the analysis and will participate in the process of designing the detection and intervention model.

Goodbye to the Mediterranean diet: nutrition and healthy habits of childhood in Spain – Adiós a la dieta mediterránea: nutrición y hábitos saludables de la infancia en España

Client: Save the Children Spain

My role: co-authorship

Language: Spain

Summary: We conducted a Spanish national survey of 2,000 families to understand the impact of the COVID-19 crisis on the nutrition and health of children in Spain. In the study, we compare the results with the National Health Survey of Spain carried out in 2017. The research shows that almost 28.1% of those under 18 years of age would be overweight and/or obese in Spain after COVID -19, a similar proportion to that before the pandemic, which stood at 27.6%. This situation can be aggravated by the increase in the prices of vegetables and fruits as a result of the recent runaway inflation.

Grow up mentally healthy – Crecer Saludable(mente)

Client: Save the Children Spain

My role: co-authorship

Language: Spanish

Summary: After conducting a Spanish national survey of 2,000 families on the mental health of their children, the report indicates that the incidence of these problems is three times higher (10%) in unemployed families than families that have kept their jobs (3%) after the COVID-19 crisis. Furthermore, children and adolescents living in low-income households are 4 times more likely (13%) to suffer from mental and/or behavioral disorders than those living in high-income households (3%). Also in the poorest households there is a greater proportion of migrant children who have a higher incidence of mental and/or behavioral disorders.
