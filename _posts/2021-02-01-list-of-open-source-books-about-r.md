I would like to share **this list of free/open source books** about R. These books teach different topics: tha basics of R, how to do graphs, statistics, econometrics... Most of these books are web-based, but some books are in pdf format. Just as a note: ***I have not used or checked of all them***. As a final comment: **I will be updating this list**.

## Start with R

- *[R for Beginners](https://cran.r-project.org/doc/contrib/Paradis-rdebuts_en.pdf)* by Emmanuel Paradis
- *[R para principiantes](https://bookdown.org/jboscomendoza/r-principiantes4/)* (*in Spanish*) by Juan Bosco Mendoza Vega
- *[Hands-On Programming with R](https://rstudio-education.github.io/hopr/)* by Garrett Grolemund
- [The Epidemiologist R Handbook](https://epirhandbook.com/en/index.html) by [Applied Epi](https://www.appliedepi.org/)
- **More advanced level**: [*Advanced R*](https://amzn.to/2WoabjB) by Chapman, & Hall’s R Series

## Graphs

- [*R Graphics Cookbook, 2nd edition*](https://r-graphics.org/index.html) by *Winston Chang*
- *[ggplot2: Elegant Graphics for Data Analysis](https://ggplot2-book.org/index.html)* by [Hadley Wickham](http://hadley.nz), [Danielle Navarro](https://djnavarro.net), & [Thomas Lin Pedersen](https://www.data-imaginist.com).
- *[Data Visualization with R](https://rkabacoff.github.io/datavis/index.html)* by Rob Kabacoff

## Data Science/Statistics/Econometrics

- **[R for Data Science](https://cran.r-project.org/doc/contrib/Paradis-rdebuts_en.pdf)** by collaborative platform
- [*Data Science in Education with R*](https://datascienceineducation.com/) by Ryan A. Estrellado, Emily A. Bovee, Jesse Mostipak, Joshua M. Rosenberg, & Isabella C. Velásquez
- [*Introduction to Econometrics with R*](https://www.econometrics-with-r.org/) by Christoph Hanck, Martin Arnold, Alexander Gerber & Martin Schmelzer[](https://datascienceineducation.com/)
- [*Library of Statistical Techniques (LOST)*](https://lost-stats.github.io/) *(they show you how to implement them with Python, R and Stata*) originated by Nick Huntington-Klein & maintained by volunteer contributors.
- *[Mastering Spark with R](https://therinspark.com/index.html)* by [Javier Luraschi](https://twitter.com/javierluraschi), [Kevin Kuo](https://twitter.com/kevinykuo) & [Edgar Ruiz](https://twitter.com/theotheredgar)
- *[R para profesionales de los datos: una introducción](https://www.datanalytics.com/libro_r/index.html)* (in Spanish) by Carlos J. Gil Bellosta
- *[Forecasting: Principles and Practice (3rd ed)](https://otexts.com/fpp3/)* by Rob J. Hyndman and George Athanasopoulos
- *[Handling Strings With R](https://www.gastonsanchez.com/r4strings/)* (*only the preview of the first four chapters*) by [Gaston Sanchez](https://www.gastonsanchez.com/)
- *[Hands-On Machine Learning with R](https://bradleyboehmke.github.io/HOML/)* by [Bradley Boehmke](https://github.com/bradleyboehmke) & [Brandon Greenwell](https://bgreenwell.netlify.app/)
- *[Modern Statistics with R](https://modernstatisticswithr.com/)* by Måns Thulin

## R Markdown

- *[R Markdown: The Definitive Guide](https://bookdown.org/yihui/rmarkdown/)* by Yihui Xie, J. J. Allaire, & Garrett Grolemund
- *[Escritura de libros con bookdown](https://rubenfcasal.github.io/bookdown_intro/)* (*in Spanish*) by Fernández-Casal, R., & Cotos-Yáñez, T.R.
- *[bookdown: Authoring Books and Technical Documents with R Markdown](https://bookdown.org/yihui/bookdown/)* by Yihui Xie
- *[Elegant, flexible, and fast dynamic report generation with R](https://yihui.org/knitr/)* by Yihui Xie
- *[R Markdown Workshop](https://resulumit.com/blog/rmd-workshop/)* by [Resul Umit](https://resulumit.com/about/)
- *[R Markdown Cookbook](https://bookdown.org/yihui/rmarkdown-cookbook/)* by Yihui Xie, Christophe Dervieux, & Emily Riederer

## Others

- *[Geocomputation with R](https://geocompr.robinlovelace.net/)* by Robin Lovelace, Jakub Nowosad, & Jannes Muenchow
- *[Mastering Software Development in R](https://bookdown.org/rdpeng/RProgDA/)* by Roger D. Peng, Sean Kross, and Brooke Anderson
- *[blogdown: Creating Websites with R Markdown](https://bookdown.org/yihui/blogdown/)* by Yihui Xie, Amber Thomas & Alison Presmanes Hill
- *[R packages](https://r-pkgs.org/)* (*work-in-progress 2nd edition*) by [Hadley Wickham](http://hadley.nz), & [Jenny Bryan](http://jennybryan.org/)
- *[Mastering Shiny](https://mastering-shiny.org/)* by [Hadley Wickham](http://hadley.nz)
